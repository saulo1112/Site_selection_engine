_Generated by `src/models/lookalike_v4.py`. Total hexagons: **3589**._

## What changes relative to v3

**Same model** (Logistic Regression) and **same validation** (spatial CV with H3 res-6 blocks + 1-ring buffer). The only new thing: **demographic** features prorated by city block are added — population and housing units (DANE CNPV 2018 census) and socioeconomic stratum (IDECA Bogotá).

Demographics that entered the model: `viviendas_estimadas`, `estrato_promedio`.

## Limitation

Still a score of **similarity / exploration priority** (not performance), with the look-alike assumption that D1's location strategy is good (docs/metodologia.md §5). Demographics add market context, they do not turn the score into a sales prediction.
